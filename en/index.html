FAQ

Q: Are my images uploaded to a server?
A: No. Everything runs in your browser. Images never leave your device, and it works offline.

Q: Can the watermark be removed afterwards?
A: No — it's drawn into the pixels, so saving is irreversible. Always keep your original and re-export from it when you need a clean version.

Q: Why should I enable the outline?
A: White text disappears against bright skies or white walls. The outline adds a semi-transparent dark edge so the mark stays readable on any background.

Q: Doesn't tiling look ugly?
A: A little. Tiling is for anti-theft (it can't be cropped away). For normal portfolio display, a single bottom-right mark at ~50% opacity looks much better.

Q: Does it reduce quality?
A: Output is high-quality JPEG (95%), visually indistinguishable. Resolution stays identical to the original.

Q: Is EXIF data preserved?
A: No. Redrawing drops EXIF metadata, which conveniently also removes location privacy data from your photos.

Q: Can I watermark multiple images at once?
A: Currently one image at a time. Batch support is in development.
