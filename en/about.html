About

A free, open-source, privacy-first online tool

Why we built this

Watermarking images is a regular need: photographers signing their work, brands marking assets, shops deterring image theft, people asserting copyright. But the usual online tools require you to upload your image to their server — and what people watermark is often unreleased original work. Uploading it means handing it to a third party first.

Worse, many free watermark tools then stamp their own watermark on top of yours. You hand over an image and get back two watermarks.

So we built this: all watermarking happens inside your browser. Your image never leaves your device. No sign-up, no fees, and no second watermark from us.

How it works

  1. Decode — the browser natively decodes your image.
  2. Draw — the image is drawn to a Canvas, and the watermark is composited on top at the chosen position (or tiled across the image). Opacity uses globalAlpha; rotation uses a coordinate transform.
  3. Export — written out as a high-quality JPEG via toBlob().

No server-side computation is involved, so it even works offline.

Why the outline option matters

White watermark text disappears against bright skies or white walls. The outline draws a semi-transparent dark edge around the glyphs, keeping the mark readable on any background — a small detail that makes a real difference.

Privacy bonus

Redrawing drops the original EXIF metadata (capture time, device, GPS location), which protects your location privacy.

Open source

Fully open source (MIT): haixiansheng/image-watermark.

Important note

Once a watermark is saved into the pixels it cannot be removed. Always keep your original and re-export from it when you need a clean version.
